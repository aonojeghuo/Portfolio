## Featured Portfolio Projects

### 1\. Iraq: Air Pollution Analysis & Spatial Risk Atlas

  * **Overview**: Interactive R Shiny dashboard and AI-generated environmental summaries analyzing atmospheric pollution hotspots (CO, NO2, SO2, PM2.5, aerosols) across Iraq.
  * **Highlights**: Automated district and governorate profiling, demographic exposure mapping, and integration of AI contextualization models.
  * **Publication**: Published in *Environmental Monitoring and Assessment* (2025). [Read Paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10661-025-14571-9)
  * **Interactive App**: [View R Shiny Dashboard](https://jolexyenviro.com/iraq-air-quality-%26-health)

### 2\. UNICEF CCRI-DRM Subnational Climate Hazard & Risk Modeling

  * **Overview**: Subnational Children's Climate Risk Index (CCRI-DRM) models and hazard exposure assessments for regions including Kenya, Tajikistan, and South Sudan (INFORM Risk Framework).
  * **Highlights**: Automated GeoAI risk indicators, multi-hazard exposure layers, and interactive analytical web applications for climate disaster resilience.

### 3\. Bike-Share Usage & Spatial Trend Analysis (Cyclistic)

  * **Overview**: Comprehensive spatial and temporal analysis of bike-share trips in Chicago using R and Tableau.
  * **Highlights**: Automated data pipeline for monthly aggregation and feature extraction, station mode calculations, interactive spatial heatmaps, and rider behavior profiling.
  * **Full Report**: [View RPubs Report](https://rpubs.com/aronojeghuo/Google-Cyclistic)

### 4\. Executive Sales & Regional Profit Analytics

  * **Overview**: Power BI Desktop and Service App solutions for global sales performance, customer value ranking, and regional profit distribution.
  * **Highlights**: 4-year trajectory modeling, regional slicers, interactive sales category breakdowns, and executive metrics dashboards.
